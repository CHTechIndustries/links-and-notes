chipset is the chips that optimize communication between other parts and the CPU
	first, everything directly hardwired to CPU. everything same priority, overwhelmed CPU and caused bottlenecks

	second, added 2 chips. North bridge (top), and south bridge (bottom). Every component went to one of those chips
	fast computers (RAM, GPU, ect.) went to North Bridge
	Slow (Hardrives, SSD, IO, Storage)
		Then, South Bridge goes to North Bridge, and the North Bridge to go to the CPU

	third, CPU became the North Bridge
		then, IO, Storage, ect. goes into "south bridge" (Intel = Platform Controller Hub), (AMBD= "another term"), which directly connects to CPU
