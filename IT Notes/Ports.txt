a standardized way of communicating data (protocol)

a port is a specific, well, port for data

0-~1023 is the common and standard ports
past that to ~50,000 are non-registered, so used by specific programs, organizations, ect.

49152 to 65535 are "dynamic" or "ephemeral". Temporarily assigned when you, say, connect to a website. Then the data from that website goes through that port
